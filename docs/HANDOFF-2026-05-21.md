## Important Architecture Decisions

- `core` is the library.
- `interfaces/cli` is only a shell.
- `src/main.rs` should use `rove::...`, not local `mod core; mod tools;`.
- Workspace is the product boundary.
- Repo is only one `WorkspaceKind`, not the whole product.
- M0-M2 may look like Claude Code at the surface. That was acceptable because they were the engine, not the final differentiation.
- Differentiation now continues through replay, observability, artifacts, run comparison, workspace memory, and workbench-style product features.
